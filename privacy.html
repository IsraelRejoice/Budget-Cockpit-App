Budget Cockpit privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026. Applies to the Budget Cockpit web app, the Android app and the iOS app.

Budget Cockpit is a personal budgeting app built around pay cycles. It is run by Onakoya Israel, an individual based in Lagos, Nigeria, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What we collect

We only hold what you enter or what the app needs to run your account.

DataWhy
Email address and password (the password is stored as a hash by our login provider, never in readable form)To create and secure your account and to send password reset emails.
Budget data you enter: income, pay cycle settings, categories and budgets, expenses with descriptions, extra income, savings, debts, loans (including names and repayment dates), household member names and tags, bills and notesTo provide the app. This is the core of the service.
Cycle history and generated reportsTo show past cycles and let you export or share a report.
Push notification address for your device (if you turn on reminders), your reminder time and time zoneTo send your daily reminder.
Chat messages you send to the Lotus assistantTo answer you and to show your chat history back to you.
Technical data such as request time and error logsTo keep the service running and fix faults.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifiers or bank login details. We do not connect to your bank. Budget Cockpit does not show ads and we do not sell your data.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the database and the login system.
  • Groq receives a summary of your current cycle (totals, category spending, budgets, bill names) and your recent chat messages when you use Lotus, so it can answer. It is not sent unless you use Lotus.
  • Resend delivers emails (reminders, reports and password resets) to the address on your account when those features are on.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that sign-in attempts come from people and not bots.
  • Your device maker's push service (Google or Apple) delivers notifications.
  • Public services provide exchange rates and a daily quote. They receive a request from your device but no budget data.

These providers process data only to deliver their part of the service. Your data may be stored and processed in countries outside Nigeria.

On your device

The app keeps a local copy of your budget so it works offline. A PIN, if you set one, is stored only on your device as a hash. Biometric unlock is handled by your device and we never receive your fingerprint or face data. Privacy mode and hide settings only change what is shown on screen.

Sharing

Your data is private to your account. If you create a share link for a report, anyone with that link can read that report until it expires after 7 days or you revoke it. If you use the household feature, the people you add are labels inside your own account and do not get their own access. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database immediately. Routine backups held by our hosting provider may keep copies for a short period before they are overwritten.

Your rights and deleting your account

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask to access, correct, export or erase your data, and to object to its processing. Most of this you can do yourself in the app. You can edit or delete any entry, export your reports, and delete your whole account at any time.

To delete your account: open the app, go to Settings, then Your data, then Delete my account. If you can no longer sign in, follow the steps on the delete account page.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each account is isolated at the database level so one person's data cannot be read by another through the app. The person who runs the service has administrative access to the database, as is true of any hosted service, and uses it only to operate and support the app.

Children

Budget Cockpit is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Not financial advice

Budget Cockpit is a tracking tool. Reports are not official bank statements, and nothing in the app or from the Lotus assistant is financial, tax or legal advice.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
