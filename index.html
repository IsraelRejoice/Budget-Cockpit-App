Guide & FAQ

How this app works

How do I get started?
Go to Settings, enter your income, your payday (day of month), then set a budget for each category. Everything else updates automatically as you log expenses.
Why does my payday move around?
Your pay date is set to a fixed day of the month (default 25th). If that date falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the cycle automatically shifts to the preceding Friday — matching how most salaries are actually paid.
Why aren't Savings/Emergency Fund counted as spending?
Money you deliberately move into savings isn't an expense — it's still yours, just earmarked. It's excluded from "Spent" and tracked separately on the Savings tab, where it accumulates toward a goal over time instead of resetting each cycle.
What's "Safe to spend per day"?
(Remaining budget) ÷ (days left until payday). It's a pace check — if you spend at or below this amount each remaining day, you'll land within budget by payday. Spending more than this on a given day just means tighter days ahead, not an emergency.
What happens when I archive a cycle?
Totals are saved to History, savings contributions fold into your lifetime accumulated total, a report is generated, and transactions/extra income reset. Category budgets and goals stay the same.
Can I share this app with someone else?
If you send them your HTML file as-is, they'd be reading and writing to your same Google Sheet — their data would mix into yours, not stay separate, and you would not get a distinct report for them. For someone else to use this properly, they need their own copy: their own Google Sheet, their own Apps Script deployment, and their own token pasted into their own copy of this file.

Notes & strategy

01
Treat irregular or contract income as temporary. If your pay isn't guaranteed long-term, avoid raising fixed costs to match a good month.
02
Build the emergency fund before extra investing. Aim for 3–6 months of essential costs somewhere liquid.
03
Log expenses the same day. The categories and pace alerts only stay useful if the data is current.
04
If your total budgeted exceeds your income, something has to give. Check the "Total budgeted" line on Dashboard and Settings — it turns red the moment your categories add up to more than you earn.
05
Review every payday. Archive the cycle, read the generated report, and adjust next cycle's budgets based on what actually happened.
