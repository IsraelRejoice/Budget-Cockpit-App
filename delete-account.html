Delete your Budget Cockpit account

Effective 21 November 2026

If you can sign in

  1. Open Budget Cockpit and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password, type DELETE and confirm.

Your login and all data in the account are deleted at once. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Budget Cockpit account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, expenses, budgets and categories, history, debts and loans, household members and tags, reminders and push subscriptions, assistant chat history and share links. Routine backups at our hosting provider may keep copies briefly until they are overwritten. Nothing is kept for any other purpose.
